Punctuations

1. Dot .

This is a dot, and I am the dot.

I created it. It’s small but functional.

My existence is reliant on the point in a sentence that determines an end.

I feel value in all that I do, because life can sometimes suggest we are always trying to attain profound endings — and mine happens all the time.

Every second of every day, I conclude all that is said and perceived.

I am happy with my life and the goals that I have been given.

I am a dot, and I am proud of who I am.

2. Question Mark ?

I am a question mark.

A dot underneath a curve.

Together we create something significant.

A moment in a sentence where you think, “What is that all about?”

And because I am a knowing thing, I can recall images that attach to other things to create meaning.

That’s when this emoji appeared 🤔

A paused finger on a chin.

……..

This is where it all began, and I became a full thing — a knowing being that had purpose and worth.

And I am very grateful. 🙏

3. And &

I am &.

I like to make words make friends. I entice two entities to join and connect as one.

Sometimes it can be challenging 😥 — and it doesn’t happen.

So, as I said, some days I don’t feel myself. You could say a little incomplete.

Over the years I’ve realised that team-building word days help.

I tend to get a lot of Colours. They’re popular team buddies. I’ve successfully joined red and blue, yellow and green together over the years — and this has been very rewarding.

4. Comma ,

I am a comma.

I’m not quite a full stop, and I tend to be splattered about in a sentence.

I’ve come to terms with the fact I’m never going to be a full stop — and I’m okay with that.

Sometimes you just have to accept who you are.

To expand further, I am perhaps the moment when you need a pause, but not fully.

When you’re in mid flow and realise you really need to breathe.

My position is always an awkward 😬 one, because it would be better if I didn’t have to be there.

But due to humans — who are biologically unfortunate in their airway department — I’m important.

And it’s okay.

I’m cool with that.

We all have our lot in life, and that’s mine.
